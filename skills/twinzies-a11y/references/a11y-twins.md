# Accessibility twin archetypes

Use as lenses. They are hypotheses built from published guidance and community accounts, not people. Reuse or adapt matching twins from the library. When evidence is thin, say so.

| Archetype | Typical setup | What to walk for |
|---|---|---|
| Keyboard-only | No mouse or pointer; may use switch or sip-and-puff devices through keyboard emulation | Focus order, visible focus, traps, hover-only controls, skip links, shortcuts |
| Screen reader user | NVDA / JAWS / VoiceOver / TalkBack, navigates by headings, landmarks, links, forms | Names and roles, heading outline, form labels, dynamic status messages, modal focus, table headers |
| Low vision and zoom | Browser zoom to 200-400%, large text, high contrast or dark mode | Reflow, truncated text, fixed heights, contrast, focus indicator size, small targets |
| Color vision difference | Red-green or blue-yellow difference; may use grayscale | Color-only meaning in charts, status, errors, links |
| Motor and tremor | Touch or mouse with limited precision, one hand, voice control | Target size and spacing, drag actions, timing, accidental activation, undo |
| Cognitive and attention | Dyslexia, ADHD, anxiety, brain fog; reads slowly or is easily interrupted | Plain language, step count, memory load, error recovery, timeouts, distraction, authentication |
| Deaf and hard of hearing | Reads captions, sign-language first | Captions, transcripts, visual alerts, text alternatives for audio cues |
| Vestibular and photosensitivity | Motion sickness, migraines, seizure risk | Parallax, auto-scroll, animation, flashing, reduced-motion support |
| Situational | Bright sun, one hand, noisy room, small screen, slow network | Contrast, target size, captions, offline and timeout behavior |

For each walkthrough record: the twin, the task, where they got stuck, the success criterion, and the fix. Pair with real participants through the twinzies research-pairing prompts when possible, and compensate disabled participants fairly.
