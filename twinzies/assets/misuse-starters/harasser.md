- Behavior: repeated unwanted contact, tagging or mentioning, reposting private content, creating new accounts after being blocked.
- What stops them: block and mute that work everywhere, privacy-first defaults, report flows that are easy for people in distress, rate limits on contact, content controls.
- Design for the target first: how does a harassed user find help in two taps, calmly, on a bad day?
